Hi, I'm Mansi Vedpathak.

I get drawn to questions, odd observations, and problems that don't quite make sense. Curiosity is usually where things start, and I like to follow it far enough to understand, experiment, and sometimes turn it into something tangible.

I study AI & Data Science, but I don't keep my interests in one box. My work moves between technology, ideas, data, design, and whatever else feels worth exploring.

I'm less interested in having a fixed direction than in finding interesting directions to follow.
